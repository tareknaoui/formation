# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TailwindCSS v4, TypeScript, Lucide React, Prisma, NextAuth

## Users
Algerian students, professionals, and language enthusiasts seeking to master Mandarin Chinese quickly and effectively for career opportunities, studying in China, or personal growth.

## Product Purpose
Provide a premium, high-converting, and accessible self-paced Mandarin learning platform ("Le Chinois Vite & Bien" / "Formation Mandarin DZ") featuring structured HSK-aligned video lessons, printable writing workbooks, lifetime 24/7 access, and direct coach mentorship.

## Positioning
The premier Algerian Mandarin learning platform combining structured video curriculum, writing workbooks, lifetime access, and local payment integration (CCP / BaridiMob via WhatsApp).

## Operating Context
High-impact web landing page and responsive learning portal accessible across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- High-converting modern brand showcase with glassmorphism & dark/luminous aesthetics.
- Video demo showcase / interactive course preview.
- Detailed Course Syllabus & HSK Curriculum Breakdown (video modules, grammar, hanzi writing).
- Coach / Teacher Introduction & Credentials section.
- Direct WhatsApp ordering flow pre-configured for "Méthode Solo (4 500 DA à vie)".
- Member portal sign-in and course access routing.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Le Chinois Vite & Bien — Formation Mandarin DZ 🇩🇿
- Colors: Deep Space Charcoal/Navy (`#0B0F19` / `#0F172A`), Vibrant Crimson Mandarin (`#EF4444` / `#FA4949`), Glassmorphic Accents, Emerald Trust Badges.
- Pricing: 4 500 DA (Single lifetime payment via CCP / BaridiMob).

## Evidence on Hand
- Existing Next.js application structure with `/courses`, `/auth`, `/admin`, and `/profile`.
- Complete database schema and seed data for course modules.

## Product Principles
1. Visual Excellence: Modern, premium dark-mode glassmorphic design with refined typography and smooth interactions.
2. Value Transparency: Clear curriculum breakdown, HSK roadmaps, and explicit deliverables.
3. Frictionless Conversion: 1-click WhatsApp registration and instant local payment guidance.
